## Typed portfolio continuation
Keep the original broker `positions`/`contracts` intact. A pure shared classifier recognizes explicit STK versus identified CASH currency pairs (account, positive conId, distinct known fiat base/quote), and exposes unsupported facts without dropping them. A negative FX-pair position is neither a stock short nor a cash balance. Stock protection and security gross notional project only the appropriate instrument class; cash/FX risk remains visible and actual funding requires its separate fresh ledger evidence. Active FX orders still block the equity-only risk calculator.

Legacy untyped executions remain unknown. New callbacks retain contract evidence; duplicate execId may only enrich missing contract fields, never change immutable economics or a known identity. Startup still requires complete native reconciliation. No cash balance or leverage permission is inferred from the FX pair fact. Official IBKR virtual FX/real balances distinction is the evidence source, not a strategy authorization. No broker order/cancel methods are added.

## Continuous entry admission continuation
The existing TradingService checks funding/daily notional before a separate AuditLog reservation; concurrent requests can therefore observe the same resources. Joint admission must own a single SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE boundary for durable intent and resource claims, revalidate current gates/funding immediately before reservation, and retain STARTED/UNKNOWN claims across restart. Native acknowledgement is not permission to release a cash claim. Only exact account/contract/permId/intent lineage plus fresh broker order/execution/cash evidence can transfer a claim to broker-accounted exposure; no orderRef-only adoption or automatic unknown replay.

Keep the callback safety owner and PaperStrategyObserver read-only. A continuous executor belongs to the separately approved strategy/API owner and remains disabled until native acceptance. Ordinary observer sizing already uses strategy.quantity100 and the effective100-share/2000USD ceilings; quantity is not forced to100 for expensive shares. A strategy signal is not a reservation, and the classic minute strategy is not a value-investing return validation.

Implement in reviewable stages: transactional daily/cash reservation, explicit native submission identity evidence and reconciliation, then continuous signal/executor integration. Preserve legacy modes and histories without silently treating legacy FAILED/staged/FX conversions as current stock cash claims. Current native partial-fill/full lifecycle acceptance remains a distinct unfinished item.
